How the chain works

Jarvis tries providers top to bottom with retries spaced against rate limits. First real answer wins. Dead model slugs are retired to a blocklist so a removed model never stalls the chain again. Free models keep it at $0; paid fallbacks exist if keys are set.

Retired slugs

Models die constantly on free tiers. These are blocklisted so the chain skips them instantly instead of burning a 404 each: nex-agi/n2.5-pro, gpt-oss-20b:free, muse-spark-zen, llama-3.3-70b-versatile, llama-3.1-8b-instant, llama-3.3-70b-instruct:free, qwen3-coder:free. Set JARVIS_MODEL to pin anything else.

Free-tier reality

Free models are free per token but rate-limited — typically ~50 requests/day shared across everyone on a keyless-tier key. That shared cap, not the bot, is what goes quiet first on busy days. Owner keys, Groq, or self-hosted Ollama lift it.
